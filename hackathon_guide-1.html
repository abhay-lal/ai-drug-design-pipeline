Objective

Design the best-scoring binder to GLP-1R — a class B GPCR and validated drug target for type 2 diabetes and obesity. Use the provided structure files and any model on Hub. The live leaderboard is at Hub → Leaderboard and updates every 30 seconds.

Track A

Biologics Peptide / Protein Binders

Design a peptide or protein that binds to GLP-1R. Use the semaglutide sequence as a starting point and mutate, extend, or redesign it.

Recommended Models
BindCraftDe novo protein binder design
BoltzGenGenerative binder & protein design

Track B

Small Molecule Ligand Binders

Design or dock a small molecule to the GLP-1R orthosteric pocket. Use LY3502970 (from 6XOX) as a scaffold or design something new.

Recommended Models
Boltz-2Protein–ligand structure prediction
DiffDockDiffusion-based docking confidence
FlowDockFlow-based docking confidence
NeuralPlexerCo-structure confidence
Boltz-2 YAML — Protein–Ligand
version: 1 sequences: - protein: id: A sequence: # paste full GLP-1R sequence from chainR files MKTIIALSYIFCLVFA...HHHHHHHH - ligand: id: B smiles: "[your molecule SMILES here]" constraints: - pocket: binder: B contacts: - [A, 152] # Y152 - [A, 306] # W306 - [A, 364] # E364 - [A, 380] # R380
LY3502970 baseline SMILES (from 6XOX):
Cc1cc(NC(=O)c2cnc(N3CCC[C@@H]3C(N)=O)nc2)ccc1F
Winner criteria: DiffDock/FlowDock/NeuralPlexer → higher confidence. Boltz-2 → lower ΔG (kcal/mol).

Tips for Success

→Track B: LY3502970 SMILES (from 6XOX) is a strong small molecule baseline. Try scaffold hopping or fragment-based modifications.
